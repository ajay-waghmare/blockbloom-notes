  - Created `OutOfCoinsDialog` modal rendered when tapping unaffordable lifelines in `LifelineBar` or the coin revive button in `GameOverDialog`.
  - Dialog details the exact shortfall (`"You need X more coins for [Lifeline]"`) and offers two clear paths:
    - **Shop Coins & Gems**: Direct shortcut into the Shop.
    - **Watch Video (+1 Free)**: Opt-in Rewarded Ad to earn +1 free item into inventory (available when ads are active).

  - Enhanced `GameState` with `previousScreen: Screen` and `outOfCoinsPrompt: OutOfCoinsPrompt?`.
