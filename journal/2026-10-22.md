# 2026-10-22: Daily In-App Rating & Review Push (vc25)

## Summary
Created release branch `release/2026-10-25` to introduce an active in-app rating and review prompt. To boost organic store ratings and written reviews on Google Play, players are prompted after successfully completing a game, strictly capped to at most once per day.

## Key Changes
1. **Branch**: `release/2026-10-25` branched from latest production-tested `release/2026-10-20`.
2. **Version Bump**:
   - `versionCode = 25`
   - `versionName = "1.12.2"`
3. **Daily Gate Logic (`ReviewPrompter.kt`)**:
   - `canPromptToday()`: Checks `todayEpochDay > lastPromptEpochDay` and `!hasRated`.
   - `markPromptShownToday()`: Enforces the once-per-day frequency cap.
   - `markRated()`: Permanently silences the prompt once the player leaves a review.
   - `openPlayStore()`: Deep links straight to Google Play Store listing (`market://details?id=com.aweagle.blockbloom`).
   - `launchInAppReview()`: Requests Google Play's in-app review flow with automatic store fallback.
4. **UI: `RatingPromptDialog.kt`**:
   - Interactive 5-star bar (defaults to 5 glowing gold stars).
   - "Rate & Review" primary button directing high-rating users directly to Google Play with custom comment.
   - "Maybe Later" secondary button.
   - Smooth animated entrance and back-handler support.
5. **Game Triggers (`GameViewModel.kt`)**:
   - **Endless Mode**: At Game Over for valid completed runs (`score >= 50` or `lines >= 1`).
   - **Adventure Mode**: On Level Victory (`finishLevel` with `success = true`).
   - **Daily Challenge Mode**: On Challenge completion (`bumpChallenge` win).
6. **Localization**:
   - Added `rating_prompt_*` localized copy across all 7 supported locales (EN, DE, ES, JA, KO, PT-BR, TR).
7. **Verification**:
   - Added `ReviewPrompterTest.kt` covering daily rollover, first-run eligibility, same-day suppression, and permanent rating suppression.
   - `./gradlew testReleaseUnitTest` passed 100% (33 tasks, 0 failures).
