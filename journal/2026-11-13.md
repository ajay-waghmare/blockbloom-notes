## Done Today
- **Root Cause Fix for Play Store Reviews**: OEM stores (like Xiaomi GetApps) were intercepting `market://` links on devices lacking explicit package targets. Added `.setPackage("com.android.vending")` across all store intents:
  - `ReviewPrompter.kt` (`openPlayStore`)
  - `InAppUpdateHost.kt` (`fallbackToPlayStore`)
  - `SettingsScreen.kt` & `UpdateRequiredOverlay.kt`
- **Unlocked Pre-Fix Players**: Added init-time migration `CURRENT_PROMPT_VERSION = 2` (`promptVersion`) in `ReviewPrompter.kt` to reset `hasRated = false` once, unlocking players who tapped "Rate" on v1.12.2 but never reached the Play Store due to OEM app store interception.
- **Removed Mid-Game Quota Consumption**: `GameViewModel.positiveMoment()` now only logs `"positive_moment"` analytics rather than silently consuming `reviewPrompter.onPositiveMoment()`, ensuring `RatingPromptDialog` actually presents at Game Over / Level Win.
- **Lowered Prompt Threshold**: In `GameViewModel.triggerGameOver()`, reduced score requirement from `>= 50` to `>= 25` (or `>= 1` line clear).
- **Direct Review CTAs Added**:
  - `HomeScreen.kt`: Gold `★` review button added to top bar next to Settings (`where = "home_top"`).
  - `Dialogs.kt`: Gold `★ Review` button added to `GameOverDialog` action bar alongside Share and Home (`where = "game_over"`).
- **Copy & Localization**: Updated `rating_prompt_desc` and `rating_prompt_rate_button` to actively push for written comments on Google Play; localized `rate_short` across all 7 app locales (`values`, `values-de`, `values-es`, `values-ja`, `values-ko`, `values-pt-rBR`, `values-tr`).
