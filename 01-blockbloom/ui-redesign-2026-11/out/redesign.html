Summary

Premium, clearer, and easier to monetise

The game feels good to play; the screens around it do not yet look or behave like a premium title. The same eight fixes apply to every direction; the directions change the look.

What holds it back today

  • Home: eleven blocks compete and there is no persistent navigation.
  • Gameplay: a banner ad under the tray, unlabelled lifelines, tiny tray pieces, a hint that never goes away.
  • Broken text: “BEST VALU E” in the Shop and “Achieveme nts”.
  • Daily challenge opens on an empty calendar.
  • Adventure is a wall of padlocks.
  • Mixed icons and low-contrast text make it look less premium than it plays.

What changes on every screen

  • One primary action per screen; bottom navigation with badges.
  • Pause menu, ghost piece, line-clear bar, combo meter in play.
  • Lifelines with names, owned counts and disabled states.
  • Today's challenge first, with a month streak track.
  • An Adventure path with regions and milestone chests.
  • Shop tabs, a featured offer and real theme cards.
  • Trophies you claim, with tiers and Claim all.
  • One icon family, a type scale, 4.5:1 contrast, RTL and long-text ready.

Recommendation

Ship 1 · Aurora Glass as the new default. It keeps the plum-and-teal identity players know and the store art you have, with the lowest build effort.

Sell 2, 3 and 4 as full-UI themes in the Shop (Royal Bloom, Neon Pulse, Petal Pop), each paired with a music mood: themes become a real reason to spend gems.

Test 4 · Petal Pop as a light default for new installs in one market.

Today's UI

The eight screens as they are now

v1.12.11 (34), captured on a phone on 19 Nov 2026.

Mixed icon styles

Emoji, filled and line icons side by side make the app look assembled rather than designed.

Low-contrast secondary text

Lilac text on purple falls below WCAG AA (4.5:1) at small sizes.

No shared component set

Four card styles and three button shapes across eight screens.

Text that breaks

“BEST VALU E”, “Achieveme nts”: fixed widths will break further in German, Arabic and the CJK locales.

Colour-only meaning

Six block colours with no shape cue; about 1 in 12 men is colour-blind.

Small touch targets

Several top-bar icons are under 48 dp.

Today's UI · audit 1 of 8

Home

Everything is on the screen and nothing leads: the first decision is hard.

  1. High

    Eleven blocks compete on one screen (Continue, New Game, three modes, Daily, Chest, three tiles, event, Remove Ads). Nothing says what to do first.

    One hero action (Continue / Play), the mode as a small segmented control, the rest as compact secondary tiles.

  2. High

    Adventure, Shop and Achievements are tiles near the bottom of a scroll. There is no persistent navigation.

    A bottom navigation bar (Home · Adventure · Shop · Trophies) with badges for rewards waiting.

  3. Medium

    “New Game” sits right under “Continue” at the same size: one wrong tap ends a run.

    A secondary icon button, with a confirm step when a run is in progress.

  4. Medium

    The mode row (three large cards plus a description line) takes about a fifth of the screen for a choice most players make once.

    Segmented control; show the best score per mode.

  5. Medium

    “Weekend Cascade · Ends today”: no time left, no call to action.

    A live countdown (4h 12m) and the ×1.5 shown on coin rewards while it runs.

  6. Medium

    The chest takes a full-width row to say “READY!”.

    A compact tile that glows when ready and shows a timer when not.

  7. Low

    An unlabelled star icon top right; currency pills have no “+” to buy more.

    Remove or label the star; add “+” to the pills (opens the Shop tab).

  8. Low

    Emoji icons (🌱 ⭐ 🔥) next to line icons; the logo art is small and static.

    One icon family; an animated hero board that respects Reduce motion.

  9. Low

    “Remove Ads” floats as a text link at the very bottom.

    Move it to the Shop (featured) and Settings.

Today's UI · audit 2 of 8

Gameplay

The board works; the frame around it costs space, attention and trust.

  1. High

    A shopping banner ad sits right under the piece tray: accidental taps, a cheap look, and an ad next to game controls is a Play policy risk.

    No banner during play. Monetise at natural breaks (rewarded continue, chest), or keep the banner above the HUD with clear separation.

  2. High

    Lifelines show only an icon and a price (40 / 75 / 60): no name, no owned count, no disabled state.

    Name + owned badge (“Free” while owned) + price, and a disabled style when coins are short.

  3. High

    Tray pieces are drawn at about a quarter of a board cell and float far below the board; an empty slot looks like a bug.

    Three fixed slots, pieces at about 65% of a cell, a dashed socket for the piece being placed, a “No space” state for pieces that cannot fit.

  4. Medium

    “Drag a block to the grid — or tap a block, then tap the grid” stays on screen all game.

    Show it in the first two games (onboarding), then hide it.

  5. Medium

    Seven items crowd the top bar, and the home button leaves the run in one tap.

    A pause button (resume, restart, sound, music, home with confirm); keep score, best, mode and the event bonus.

  6. Medium

    No placement preview and no hint of which lines will clear.

    A ghost piece under the finger and a glowing bar on the rows or columns that will clear, with the points.

  7. Low

    Empty cells are close to the board colour; colour alone tells blocks apart.

    An inner bevel on empty cells; optional colour-blind patterns.

  8. Low

    No combo feedback while playing.

    A combo meter under the score (“Combo ×3 · +60 next clear”).

Foundations

Twelve principles behind every screen

01One primary action

Every screen has one obvious next step; everything else is visibly secondary.

02Persistent navigation

A bottom bar for Home · Adventure · Shop · Trophies, with badges for rewards waiting.

03Design tokens

One source for colour, type, radius, elevation and motion. Each direction in this document is one token set.

04Type scale

Display 40 / 32 / 24 · Title 18 · Body 15 · Caption 12. Numbers use tabular figures so scores do not jump.

05Contrast & colour

Text at least 4.5:1. Never colour alone: colour-blind patterns as an option.

06One icon family

Line icons 24 dp, 2 px stroke; illustrated art only for currencies and rewards.

07Motion

150–300 ms, ease-out. Line-clear burst, combo shake, coins fly to the counter. Reduce motion turns it down.

08Haptics & sound

Light tick on place, medium on clear, strong on combo ×3+. Intensity setting; sounds follow the music mood.

09Accessibility

48 dp targets, text scaling to 130%, TalkBack labels, left-handed tray option.

1013 languages

Arabic mirrors right-to-left; leave 30% room for longer text; never a fixed-width label; CJK fallback fonts.

11Fair monetisation

Rewarded ads opt-in and labelled with their length; no ads next to controls; interstitials only at natural breaks, capped; restore purchases.

12Performance

60 fps on low-end phones: no live blur below Android 12, block art pre-rendered once per colour and size.

Foundations

The new UI on every screen

These changes are in all four directions. The direction pages that follow describe only what is different in the look.

1Home

One primary action (Continue), modes as a segmented control, Daily and Chest as tiles, a live event countdown, bottom navigation with badges.

2Gameplay

Pause instead of Home, ghost piece and line-clear bar, three fixed tray slots with a “No space” state, labelled lifelines with owned counts; no banner near the tray.

3Settings

Grouped sections, mood cards with preview and lock price, accessibility toggles, cloud save and restore purchases, chevrons with current values.

4Daily challenge

Today's challenge first (goal, reward, timer, best attempt), a month streak track with milestone rewards, calendar states with a legend.

5Lucky chest

A clear “free with a short video” path plus a free timer path, labelled rewards, close in the corner.

6Adventure

A path through named regions, stars per level, a milestone chest, and a level card with goal, moves and reward.

7Shop

Tabs, a featured offer with its saving, theme cards with previews and clear states, ribbons that never wrap, free coins with a daily limit.

8Trophies

A summary with Claim all, claimable items first with a Claim button, tier medals, progress with numbers, no padlocks on items in progress.

Jetpack Compose notes

BloomTokens

A data class of colours, type, shapes, elevations and motion, provided through a CompositionLocal. One instance per direction.

BlockStyle

A sealed interface with Jelly (Aurora), Gem (Royal), NeonTube (Neon) and Clay (Petal Pop); each draws on Canvas and is cached as an ImageBitmap per colour and size.

Components

CurrencyPill · PrimaryCta · ModeSegmented · FeatureTile · EventStrip · BloomBottomNav · LifelineButton · TraySlot · ProgressTrack · RewardChip · ClaimRow.

Glass without blur

RenderEffect blur exists only from Android 12 (API 31): use layered gradients for glass so older phones look the same.

Motion & haptics

Keep the existing reduce-motion hook; add HapticFeedback with an intensity setting.

RTL & long text

LayoutDirection.Rtl for Arabic (mirror the nav and progress), flexible rows, ellipsis only as a last resort.
