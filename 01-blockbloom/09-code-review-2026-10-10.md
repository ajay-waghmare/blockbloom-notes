# Code review — vc21 release check and whole-project review (10 Oct 2026)

Scope: the three UI commits that make up vc21 (`e750a49`, `ea439be`, `c3ba49c`), the live
release on Google Play, and the whole app (9,167 lines of Kotlin in `app/src/main`, 826 lines of
tests). Method: read the diffs and every core file, ran lint, re-built the debug APK from the
shipped commit, drove it on the `bloomtest` emulator at 412 dp, 360 dp, 360 dp with 1.3× font and
in landscape, read the live Play track and release notes, and pulled GA4 for the new version.

## 2. The UI changes — opinion

They are good. The app reads as a noticeably more finished product than vc20, and the one
structural change (Play above the cards) is the right call: the thing a returning player wants is
the first thing under their thumb. The 3D block render is restrained enough not to look like a
casino, the combo pill escalation gives the streak a visible shape, the gold line-preview for
multi-clears is a clear teaching signal, and the share/home pills at game over finally have real
touch targets.

Four things to watch or tidy:

1. **Per-frame cost of `drawBlock`.** It now does four rounded rects with two gradient brushes
   per block, allocated on every draw. During a drag the board (up to 64 blocks), the tray and the
   floating piece redraw every frame, so that is ~140 shader allocations a frame where there used
   to be three flat fills. Fine on the emulator and on your phone; the risk is dropped frames on
   the 2–4 GB phones that make up the IN/ID/BR audience. Cheap mitigation: build the two brushes
   once per (colour, size) and draw inside `translate(o.x, o.y)`, or keep the face flat and only
   gradient the gloss. Measure on a low-end device before deciding.
2. **The hero showcase's floating animation ignores reduce-motion** and runs forever on Home.
   vc20's release note promised the setting is honoured; `HeroBlocks` should check
   `LocalReduceMotion` like GameScreen does. (Its amplitude is also ±3.5 raw px, about 1 dp, so
   it is nearly invisible; if a float is wanted, give it dp.)
3. **The Daily Challenge card now sits below the fold** on a Pixel-6-height screen. Deliberate
   trade-off for Play-first, but watch `daily_challenge_done` (12 users in the last 28 days) for
   a drop over the next two weeks.
4. **English leaks remain** despite the "complete translations" note: `COMBO ×N`, `Level N: …`,
   `N moves`, `+N coins`, `N gems` in the shop, about eight ViewModel toasts (`Need N more
   coins`, `+N gems restored`, `Reward claimed…`, `Daily Challenge complete!…`, `Event bonus…`),
   the calendar's month names (its comment still says the UI is English-only), and every
   reminder/push notification string and channel name. Roughly 15 strings; an afternoon.
   The large-font case: Android 14+ scales 42 sp text non-linearly, which is why the title still
   fit at 1.3×; on Android 13 and below with a large font and a 360 dp screen it would wrap
   mid-word. Giving the brand title a dp-based size (it is a logo, not body text) removes that.

## 3. Whole-project review

**Verdict:** this is a well-built codebase for a solo project. The seams (`AdHost`,
`BillingHost`, `Analytics`, `RemoteConfigSource`) are real and used, every risky path fails open,
currency has a single write path with a ledger, analytics has a build-breaking test for unwired
events, and the comments explain *why* rather than *what*. The genuine defects are few and all in
paths that are dormant today. The debts are a 1,700-line ViewModel, zero tests on the economy, and
the localisation leftovers above.

Findings, most important first. Effort: S = under an hour, M = half a day, L = a day or more.

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High (latent, ads path) | **`adLoading` never clears when a rewarded ad is dismissed before the reward.** `AdMobAdHost.showRewarded` reports only `onReward` / `onFail`; `onAdDismissedFullScreenContent` just reloads. `GameViewModel.runRewarded` sets `adLoading = true` and clears it only in those two callbacks, so the "Loading ad…" scrim stays on screen until the next ad attempt. Dormant while `ADS_ENABLED=false`; it will bite on the revive flow the day ads turn on | clear loading in `onAdShowedFullScreenContent`; treat dismiss-without-reward as a silent no-op | S |
| 2 | High (product) | **The banner is wired, not off.** `AdBanner` is mounted under the tray in `GameScreen` (line 442), gated on `adsActive`. The handover and memory said it had no call sites; that was wrong (the composable is named `AdBanner`, the file `BannerAd.kt`). With "few, non-intrusive ads" as the brief it must be removed or put behind a Remote Config key before `ADS_ENABLED` flips | delete the call or gate it | S |
| 3 | Medium (dead feature) | **`FirebaseEventConfig` is never instantiated.** The ViewModel uses `LocalEventConfig`; `BlockBloomApp` even says so in a comment. Remote Config `live_events` is fetched every hour and ignored, so LiveOps A/B events cannot run | wire `FirebaseEventConfig(LocalEventConfig(...))` in the ViewModel (one line) or delete the class | S |
| 4 | Medium (layout) | **Not locked to portrait.** No `screenOrientation` on the activity; `GameScreen` is a non-scrolling Column with a `fillMaxWidth().aspectRatio(1f)` board, which cannot fit a 360 dp-tall landscape (Home scrolls and was verified; the game screen was not reachable in landscape on the emulator, so this is by construction, not observed) | `android:screenOrientation="userPortrait"` on `MainActivity` | S |
| 5 | Medium (diagnostics) | **Release stack traces have no line numbers.** `proguard-rules.pro` has no `-keepattributes SourceFile,LineNumberTable`; the Crashlytics mapping upload de-obfuscates names only. The first real crash report will be hard to read | add the two attributes (and `-renamesourcefileattribute SourceFile`) | S |
| 6 | Medium (perf) | `drawBlock` gradient allocation per frame (section 2, item 1) | cache brushes or flatten the face | S–M |
| 7 | Low (i18n) | Hardcoded strings listed in section 2, item 4; `MONTH_NAMES` in English; notification texts and channel names in English; three `MissingTranslation` lint errors for `app_name`/`block`/`bloom` that should be `translatable="false"` | move to resources; mark brand strings non-translatable | M |
| 8 | Low (tooling) | **`lintDebug` fails** (4 errors), so lint cannot be a gate. 71 warnings: `UseKtx` ×40, dependency versions ×12, `PluralsCandidate` ×9 (the known `<plurals>` debt), `PrivateResource` ×7 (a string named `selected` shadows a Compose private resource), one each `ModifierParameter` (TraySlot), `RedundantLabel`, `ObsoleteSdkInt`, `MissingPermission` (`BloomMessagingService.notify` without a permission check; `WinBackReceiver` does it right) | fix the 4 errors, rename `selected`, then run lint per release | S |
| 9 | Low (tests) | **No tests on the money path.** `Wallet`/`Ledger` (earn, spend, cap at 500), the saved-game encode/decode round-trip (including old blobs), `reviveBoard`, and `freshTray` fairness have none; the roadmap itself called economy tests blocking before IAP goes live | add four focused JUnit classes | L |
| 10 | Low (architecture) | `GameViewModel` is 1,703 lines: navigation, run engine, levels, quests, challenge, achievements, lifelines, persistence, ads, billing and analytics in one class. Readable because it is sectioned and commented, but the next feature should land in a collaborator (a run controller for place/persist, a meta-progress class for quests/achievements/levels) rather than here. `levelStarsMap()` re-parses the CSV on every call (24× per `levelStates()`), harmless at this size | split when the next feature arrives, not before | L |
| 11 | Low (persistence) | The saved-game blob has no format version; new fields are appended and read with `getOrNull`, which works until a field is ever reordered. The prefs file also carries the ~65 KB ledger and is Auto-Backed-up; `ads_removed` restored from a backup grants the entitlement without Play (acceptable at this scale) | prefix a version number before the next blob change | S |
| 12 | Nit | `toast("Need N more coins")` in three places duplicates the existing `need_more_coins_for` resource; `interstitialCountdown` starts at 2 then resets to 3 (intended?); `GemPack` placeholder prices are never shown (fine) | tidy | S |

Security and privacy: no secrets in the tree; `AD_ID` and `INTERNET` are declared and disclosed;
receiver and service are `exported=false`; backup rules cover only the prefs file; Firebase comes
up dark by manifest and is enabled in `BlockBloomApp` only when the flag is on. Nothing to fix.

Things that are right and should stay that way: `AnalyticsTest` failing the build on unwired
events; the single currency write path and ledger; consent before SDK init; consume-before-grant
for gems; the fail-open update gate with a 0 default; `traySolvable` with a node budget that fails
open; coaching by playing instead of a card deck; reduce-motion on blooms and particles; TalkBack
labels on score and coins; deterministic daily content; idempotent rewards; the `constraints {}`
trick for the fragment bump; BOM characters removed from four source files in vc21.

## 4. What this review did not cover

- Landscape on the game screen (finding 4 is from reading, not from a screenshot).
- Frame timing on a low-end device (finding 6 is a risk, not a measurement).
- `ShopScreen`, `SettingsScreen`, `LevelsScreen`, `CalendarScreen`, `StatsScreen`,
  `OnboardingOverlay`, `Skins.kt`, `Levels.kt`, `Quests.kt`, `DailyChallenge.kt` were only
  skimmed through greps; nothing in the greps flagged them.
