# Skill 05 — Organic growth and store (ASO) research, Tier-1

The plan's levers are in `plan-2026-11/06-organic-tier1.md`. This skill is **how to find the next lever and test it**
without paid tools.

## 1. Store keywords (Google Play search)

1. **List the words people type.** Use the Play Store web search for the US (`https://play.google.com/store/search?q=
   block%20puzzle&c=apps&gl=us&hl=en_US`) and the app's search box on a phone. Write down the autocomplete
   suggestions for 10 seed words: block puzzle, relaxing puzzle, offline puzzle, brain game, 8x8, combo puzzle, flower
   puzzle, garden puzzle, daily puzzle, no wifi games.
2. **Study the top 10 results for each seed word:** which words repeat in titles and short descriptions. Never copy
   their text; never use a competitor's name.
3. **Seasonal words:** Google Trends (US, past 5 years) for "christmas puzzle game", "winter puzzle", "new year game".
4. **Pick 5–8 long-tail phrases** we can win (fewer big games use them): put one in the title (today `Block Bloom:
   Block Puzzle Game`, 30/30), 1–2 in the short description, and each 2–3 times in natural sentences in the full
   description. No keyword lists.
5. **Change one thing, then wait 7–14 days** and read Play Console store performance (US visitors → installs, search
   traffic). Keep the change or roll it back. Log every listing change with its date in the journal.

**Policy limits for listing text** (Play metadata policy, `sources.md`):
- the title is 30 characters at most;
- no emoji, emoticons or repeated special characters in the title, icon or developer name;
- nothing there that claims store performance, ranking, price or a promotion (Google's examples: "#1", "Popular";
  avoid "best" and "free" too);
- no spammy, repeated or unrelated keywords;
- no claims the app does not keep on every live version ("no ads");
- no misleading screenshots.

## 2. Store page tests

- **Store listing experiments** (icon, screenshots, short description) need traffic. With a few hundred visitors a
  week, run one experiment at a time on the default listing (all countries) and allow 3–4 weeks. If Play shows "not
  enough data", keep the current version.
- **Custom store listing** for US/UK/CA/AU carries the Tier-1 copy (C-16).
- **Promo video** (YouTube, ads off) on the listing — real gameplay, the best clear in the first seconds.

## 3. Play event cards (Promotional content)

Rules (C-19, Play Console Help):
- submit at least 4 days ahead, and 14+ days ahead to be eligible for featuring;
- at most 4 weeks long;
- a unique image, tagline and description for each event;
- the in-app event starts the same day.

Plan one event card every 2–4 weeks; each card is a free visibility moment and a reason for lapsed players to return.
Measure installs and returning players on the card's first 3 days against the 7 days before.

## 4. Short video (Shorts, Reels, TikTok)

1. **Research:** search each platform for "block puzzle", "satisfying puzzle", "puzzle game"; sort by recent. Note
   what the most-viewed clips from the last month do: the hook in the first second, length, text on screen, sound.
2. **Post 1 a day** at US evening (06:30 IST); real gameplay only.
3. **Measure weekly:** views → profile visits → `utm_source=shorts` installs. Keep the format of the top 3 clips; drop
   the bottom 3.
4. One honest "I made this game" clip a week tends to get comments; answer every comment.

## 5. Communities (Reddit) and creators

- **Reddit:** a person reads each subreddit's rules (automated reads were blocked on 22 Nov). Developer posts must be
  honest ("I made this; it has ads and one purchase"); ask for feedback, never for installs or ratings; one sub per
  day; reply to every comment. Track with `utm_source=reddit&utm_campaign=<sub>_<date>`.
- **Creators:**
  - Search YouTube for "new mobile games this week android" and "relaxing puzzle games android"; filter to this
    month; keep channels with 1k–50k subscribers that show puzzle games in the US / UK.
  - Take the business email from the About page.
  - Log each one: channel, subscribers, email, date sent, reply, post, installs (`utm_source=creator`).
  - Send in batches of 10 with the template in `06` §5. Never pay for a review without an ad disclosure, and never
    for installs.

## 6. Players bring players; players stay

- Share card and referral (V41-04): measure shares per game over, and installs from `referral`.
- Retention is a store signal: Play ranks apps people keep and do not uninstall. Return rates, crashes and ANRs are
  part of organic growth.

## 7. What is never allowed

Bought or rewarded ratings, review swaps or review farms, incentivised installs, install exchanges, fake engagement,
bots, competitor names in metadata, misleading claims. Any of these can get the app removed, and that ends all
growth.
